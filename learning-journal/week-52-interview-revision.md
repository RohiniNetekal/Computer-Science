## Interview Revision

### Short explanation
Revision means explaining concepts, solving small problems and connecting design decisions to real backend scenarios.

Requirement -> API -> Service -> DB

### Key point
Remember: Practice explaining why you chose an approach, its complexity, failure modes and alternatives.

### Interview check
- What is it?
- Why would you use it?
- What is one common mistake or trade-off?
